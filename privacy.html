Mizan Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Applies to the Mizan web app, in a browser or installed to your home screen

Mizan has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The only time your data leaves your device is when you choose to back it up — either to a file you save yourself, or to a private folder in your own Google Drive. We never receive, see, sell or share your data.

1. What Mizan stores on your device

To work, Mizan saves the following in your device’s app storage. It is not sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your profile: name (optional), age or date of birth, sex, height, weight, body measurements, activity level and goals.
  • Your routines and timetable (including plans for specific days), check-ins, habits, reflections, water, food and workout logs, meal and workout plans, and walk or run history with routes, records and badges.
  • Your conversation with the assistant, Mizo, so it can pick up where you left off. It is answered on your device — nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • Your settings, including how you customised the app and the assistant, your step-length calibration, and the safety contact (name and phone number) if you add one.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings → Backup & restore → Erase everything. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser’s site data also removes it from that device.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose Connect Google Drive, Mizan asks Google for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This lets Mizan create and update its own backup file in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive.

  • Mizan cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, photos or documents.
  • The backup goes directly from your device to your Google Drive. It never passes through a server run by us.
  • You can disconnect at any time in Settings, delete the backup from there, or remove Mizan’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Mizan’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Drive is used only to restore your own Mizan data, is not used for advertising, and is not transferred to anyone.

3. Backup files

You can also save a backup file and keep it wherever you like (for example Files, WhatsApp, email, Google Drive or iCloud Drive). If you add a password, the file is encrypted on your device before it is saved. Anyone who has the file (and its password, if you set one) can read it, so keep it somewhere safe.

Phone alarms (calendar file): if you choose Phone alarms, Mizan makes a calendar file with the names and times of your blocks so your phone’s calendar can remind you while Mizan is closed. It goes only where you put it. Once you import it into a calendar (for example Google Calendar or iCloud), that calendar’s provider stores those events under its own privacy policy. Deleting the “Mizan” calendar removes them.

4. Location

Mizan uses your location only when you ask it to:

  • Walk and run tracking: while the Run & walk screen is open (to show whether GPS is ready) and while a session is running, Mizan reads your GPS position to measure distance, pace and time. Your route is stored only on your device.
  • Sunrise and sunset times: if you turn these on, your approximate location is saved on your device to calculate them.

Mizan does not track your location in the background and never sends it anywhere by itself. Your location leaves the device only when you send it:

  • Share my location / Get help: Mizan writes a message with a map link (Google Maps) to your current position and opens your messages or share menu. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and it goes only to the people you choose.
  • GPX files: if you export a walk or run, the file contains its route and times. You decide where it goes (for example Strava or Garmin Connect).

5. Camera and photos

The skin, face care and grooming guide can use a photo you take or choose. The photo is analysed on your device to estimate things like face shape. It is not uploaded and not saved — it is kept only while that screen is open. The face-detection model is part of Mizan and runs on your device.

6. Motion sensors, sound and notifications

During a walk or run, Mizan may use your phone’s motion sensor to count steps. This happens on your device only.

Water and timetable reminders, the Couch to 5K coach and the safety alarm play sounds, vibrate and speak using your device. If you turn on notifications, they are created on your device while Mizan is running; Mizan has no server that sends them.

7. What Mizan does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no third-party trackers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No uploading of your health, fitness, location or photo data to any server run by us.

The Mizan website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) when the site is loaded, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. Google’s sign-in code is loaded from Google only if you choose to connect Google Drive. Links you choose to open (such as a helpline website) are other services with their own policies.

8. Health information

Mizan gives general wellness guidance based on public health sources. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before making major changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you have a health condition.

9. Children and teenagers

Mizan can be used by teenagers and uses age-appropriate guidance for under-18s. Because Mizan does not collect personal data on any server, we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are under 18, please use Mizan with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, especially before connecting a Google account or following diet or exercise advice.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app.
